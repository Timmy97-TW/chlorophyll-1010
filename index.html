施用時機四種（無、前處理、同時處理、後處理），NaCl 三個濃度（0、75、100 mM），共 12 組。Four application timings (none, pre, co, post) crossed with three NaCl levels (0, 75, 100 mM), 12 groups.

結果Results

同時處理降低葉綠素。相對於同鹽濃度的對照，同時處理組為 −56%（0 mM）、−51%（75 mM）、−62%（100 mM）。沒有加鹽時降幅一樣大，所以這個效應來自 BoPep4 的同時施用本身，和鹽逆境無關。Co-treatment lowered chlorophyll. Relative to the control at the same NaCl level, co-treated groups were −56% (0 mM), −51% (75 mM) and −62% (100 mM). The deficit is as large without salt, so it comes from co-application itself and does not depend on salt stress.

對照組沒有鹽效應。對照組在 0／75／100 mM 為 0.489／0.500／0.525 mg·g⁻¹，幾乎是平的。葉綠素沒有下降，就沒有空間看出保護效果。鮮重在 100 mM 下降 43%（0.311 → 0.177 g），但每組只有一個重量。The control showed no salt effect. Control values were 0.489, 0.500 and 0.525 mg·g⁻¹ at 0, 75 and 100 mM. With no decline to prevent, a protective effect has nothing to show against. Fresh weight fell 43% at 100 mM (0.311 → 0.177 g), from one weighing per group.

前處理與後處理沒有一致趨勢。前處理相對對照為 +8%、−10%、−9%；後處理為 −22%、+24%、−5%。後處理 75 mM 的高值（0.619）對應的鮮重只有相鄰兩組的一半（0.172 對 0.326、0.329 g）。Pre- and post-treatment showed no consistent trend. Pre-treatment was +8%, −10%, −9% against control; post-treatment −22%, +24%, −5%. The high post-treatment value at 75 mM (0.619) coincides with a fresh weight half that of its neighbours (0.172 vs 0.326 and 0.329 g).

飽和對結論的影響。飽和會壓低高讀值，所以對照、前處理、後處理的真實值可能更高。同時處理組的讀值在 0.65–1.01，是唯一落在線性範圍附近的處理，因此 −51 至 −62% 是降幅的下限。What saturation does to the conclusion. Saturation compresses high readings, so the true control, pre- and post-treatment values may be higher. The co-treated groups read 0.65–1.01, the only treatment near the linear range, so −51 to −62% is a lower bound on the deficit.

實驗二 · 土耕 · 300 mM NaClExperiment 2 · Soil · 300 mM NaCl

ACC deaminase × NaClACC deaminase × NaCl

五組：TB 培養基對照（0 與 300 mM），以及 300 mM 下的 ACCD 前處理、同時處理、後處理。所有讀值都在線性範圍內（A663 0.16–0.52），Chl a/b 為 1.78–2.51。Five groups: TB-medium control at 0 and 300 mM, and ACCD pre-, co- and post-treatment at 300 mM. All readings are within the linear range (A663 0.16–0.52); Chl a/b is 1.78–2.51.

結果Results

鹽效應來自分母。TB 對照在 300 mM 的每公克葉綠素比 0 mM 低 40%（0.253 對 0.418 mg·g⁻¹）。但兩管萃取液的葉綠素總量幾乎相同（27.1 對 27.6 µg），差別是加鹽組的樣本比較重（0.107 對 0.066 g）。The salt effect comes from the denominator. Per-gram chlorophyll in the TB control was 40% lower at 300 mM than at 0 mM (0.253 vs 0.418 mg·g⁻¹). The two extracts held nearly the same total chlorophyll (27.1 vs 27.6 µg); the salted sample simply weighed more (0.107 vs 0.066 g).

同時處理的「回復」也是。同時處理組每公克為 0.383，是未逆境對照的 92%。這一管只有 22 mg 組織、8.4 µg 葉綠素，是五組中最少的。So does the apparent rescue by co-treatment. The co-treated group reads 0.383 mg·g⁻¹, 92% of the unstressed control. That tube held 22 mg of tissue and 8.4 µg of chlorophyll, the smallest sample of the five.

判讀Interpretation

五組的每公克葉綠素與鮮重的相關係數為 r = −0.85。在取樣量差到 9 倍、每組只秤一次的情況下，無法區分「葉綠素濃度不同」和「取樣量不同」。要回答 ACCD 的問題，需要知道每管取了幾株、哪些葉片，並把取樣量固定下來。Across the five groups, per-gram chlorophyll correlates with fresh weight at r = −0.85. With a ninefold spread in sample mass and one weighing per group, a difference in chlorophyll concentration cannot be separated from a difference in how much tissue was taken. Answering the ACCD question needs the number of plants and which leaves went into each tube, with sample mass held fixed.

設計上還缺兩個對照：不加鹽的 ACCD 組，以及不表現 ACCD 的 WB800N 上清液。Two controls are still missing from the design: ACCD without salt, and supernatant from WB800N that does not express ACCD.

結論的範圍Scope of the conclusions

現在可以說和不能說的What the data support, and what they do not

可以說Supported

  • BoPep4 同時處理的葉綠素低於對照，三個鹽濃度方向一致，降幅至少一半。BoPep4 co-treatment gave lower chlorophyll than control at all three NaCl levels, by at least half.
  • 0–100 mM NaCl 在這個系統裡沒有降低對照組的每公克葉綠素。NaCl at 0–100 mM did not lower per-gram chlorophyll in the control in this system.
  • 土耕 ACCD 的萃取條件落在線性範圍內，a/b 也接近合理值，這套萃取量可以沿用。The soil ACCD extracts were within the linear range with near-plausible a/b, so that extraction loading can be kept.

還不能說Not supported

  • BoPep4 提高耐鹽性。對照組沒有出現鹽造成的葉綠素下降。That BoPep4 improves salt tolerance. The control showed no salt-induced loss to protect against.
  • ACCD 同時處理回復了葉綠素。這個數值來自 22 mg 的樣本。That ACCD co-treatment restored chlorophyll. The value rests on a 22 mg sample.
  • 任何顯著性。每組的生物重複是 1。Any statistical significance. Each group has one biological replicate.
  • BoPep4 實驗的絕對數值。12 組中有 10 組超出線性範圍。Absolute values in the BoPep4 experiment. Ten of twelve groups were out of range.
